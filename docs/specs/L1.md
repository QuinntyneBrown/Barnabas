# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Barnabas is a private lending, giving, selling, and helping board scoped to a church
congregation. Members post something to lend, give away, or sell, or offer their time —
a ride, tutoring, help moving. A member asks for something by making a request; if the
owner accepts, the two arrange the handoff between themselves and meet in person.

One system serves many congregations. A congregation never sees another's members,
listings, requests, or messages.

**Product boundaries.** Barnabas brokers the introduction and nothing else. It shall not
process payments, arrange shipping or delivery, hold deposits, or manage returns. These
are stated as requirements, not as omissions, because they are the product's shape.

---

## L1-001: Congregation provisioning and configuration
The system shall support many congregations on one deployment. An administrator shall be
able to provision a congregation, give it a display name and its set of neighbourhoods,
and designate the members who moderate it.

## L1-002: Invitation and joining
Access shall be by invitation only. A congregation's leaders shall be able to issue invite
codes, and a prospective member shall be able to redeem one, supply a profile, and wait
for a moderator to approve them before they can see the board.

## L1-003: Authentication and session
A member shall be able to sign in without a password, by requesting a single-use link sent
to their email address and exchanging it for a session. A member shall be able to sign out,
and a session shall expire.

## L1-004: Member profiles
Every member shall have a profile carrying their name, neighbourhood, a short description
in their own words, and the kinds of help they are willing to offer. Members shall be able
to edit their own profile and view any other member's.

## L1-005: Creating listings
A member shall be able to post a listing of one of four kinds — Lend, Give, Sell, or Help.
The four are not interchangeable: each collects the information its kind actually needs,
and a member shall choose the kind before entering details.

## L1-006: Listing lifecycle
A member shall be able to edit, close out, archive, restore, and permanently delete their
own listings. Closing out shall record how the listing ended, in the vocabulary of its kind.

## L1-007: Browsing the board
A member shall be able to browse every current listing in their congregation on a single
board, and filter it to one kind. The board shall communicate a listing's kind without
relying on colour alone.

## L1-008: Search and filtering
A member shall be able to search listings by words in the title and description, and narrow
results by kind, neighbourhood, and price.

## L1-009: Requests
A member shall be able to request a listing, writing a message to its owner and supplying
the details that kind of request needs. The owner shall be able to accept or decline. An
accepted request shall open a place for the two members to arrange the handoff.

## L1-010: Coordination messaging
Every message thread shall arise from a request on a listing, so that a conversation always
has a subject. Members shall be able to read their threads, send messages, and reach the
listing a thread concerns. The system shall not offer free-form messaging between members.

## L1-011: Notifications
A member shall be notified when something happens that needs their attention — a request on
their listing, a decision on their request, a new message, or a moderator's action. Each
notification shall lead to the thing it concerns, and a member shall be able to control which
notifications they receive.

## L1-012: Congregation directory
A member shall be able to browse the other members of their congregation, see what help each
offers, and open any member's profile.

## L1-013: Moderation
A member shall be able to report a listing. Moderators shall be able to review reported
listings, remove them, and approve or decline prospective members. A reporter's identity
shall not be disclosed to the member reported.

## L1-014: Tenant isolation
Data belonging to one congregation shall never be readable or writable by a member of
another, through any route, including by guessing an identifier. The system shall not
disclose that a resource exists in another congregation.

## L1-015: Security and data protection
The system shall authenticate every request, authorise every action against the actor's role
and ownership, validate all input, and protect members' personal information. It shall defend
against the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-016: Performance and scale
The system shall stay responsive at the scale of a congregation and across many congregations
on one deployment, with stated limits on response time and page weight.

## L1-017: Responsive layout and accessibility
Every screen shall be usable on any viewport from a small phone to a large desktop, and
usable by keyboard and assistive technology. Content shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## L1-018: Observability and operations
The system shall expose health, logs, and metrics sufficient to diagnose a failure in
production without reproducing it, and shall not leak personal information into telemetry.
