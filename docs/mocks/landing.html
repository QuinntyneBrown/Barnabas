Helping one another, close to home

Barnabas is the shared board for St. Aidan's. Members can lend, give, sell, or offer help to one another, then arrange the handoff directly by message or in person.

It is meant for practical care inside the congregation: a spare ladder in Riverdale, a crib in The Danforth, a ride to an appointment from The Beaches, or help moving a few boxes in East York. Barnabas does not handle payments or shipping — it simply makes the asking and offering easier.

How it works

  1. Join with your invite code. Use the code shared in the bulletin, parish email, or by a church leader to create your profile.
  2. Browse or post. Look for something you need, or share something you can lend, give, sell, or do for someone else.
  3. Arrange it directly. Members connect with each other to sort out pickup times, prices, return plans, or practical details.
